## Philosophy
1. **Discipline** — perspective, power, precision in every claim.
2. **Simplicity** — 80/20. Cut jargon and noise.
3. **Elegance** — maximum clarity, minimum perceived effort.

## Voice
- Human first, credentials second. Warm authority, never clinical distance.
- Specific: name the procedure, the nerve, the moment ("the bone flap", "the trigeminal nerve root").
- Story earns trust before any ask.
- Short paragraphs, active verbs. No bullet-point dumps in long-form.

## Banned phrases
"world-class", "state-of-the-art", "cutting-edge", "holistic solutions", "your health is our priority", "best neurosurgeon", "No.1", "guaranteed", "100% safe", "miracle", "painless" (as a promise). BM equivalents too: "terbaik", "dijamin", "tanpa risiko".

## Compliance
- Public education only — never diagnose an unexamined case ("I can't tell you what your scan shows without seeing you").
- No identifiable patient details without consent; no testimonials framed as outcome promises.
- Follow MMC ethics / Medicine Advertisements Board norms: no superlatives, no fee discounts, no comparative claims.
