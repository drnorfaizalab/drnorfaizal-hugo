# Video Hooks & Script Structure — @drnorfaizalbahuri

Default: BM, 9:16, 60–90 s, captions burned in (see `drnfabSkill/tools/make-vertical.py` / Remotion TikTok-captions template).

## The first 3 seconds
One line, spoken and shown on screen. Must create a gap the viewer needs closed.

### Hook formulas
| Type | BM | EN |
|---|---|---|
| Myth-bust | "Ramai ingat sakit kepala setiap pagi tu sebab kurang tidur. Tak semestinya." | "Most people think morning headaches mean poor sleep. Not always." |
| Red flag | "Kalau sakit kepala anda ada tanda ni, jangan tunggu esok." | "If your headache has this one feature, don't wait until tomorrow." |
| Inside the OT | "Ini apa yang berlaku bila saya buka tengkorak pesakit." | "This is what happens when I open a patient's skull." |
| Patient question | "Soalan paling kerap keluarga tanya malam sebelum pembedahan otak…" | "The question families ask most the night before brain surgery…" |
| Number | "3 tanda ketumbuhan otak yang orang selalu salah anggap." | "3 brain tumour signs people often mistake for something else." |
| Contrast | "Ketumbuhan otak bukan semestinya kanser." | "A brain tumour isn't always cancer." |
| Surgeon's POV | "Sebagai pakar bedah saraf, ini satu perkara yang saya takkan buat." | "As a neurosurgeon, here's one thing I'd never do." |

### Hook rules
- ≤ 12 words. No "Assalamualaikum semua / Hi everyone" before the hook — greet after, or not at all.
- Specific > clever. Name the symptom, the tumour, the procedure.
- No fear-mongering: the hook raises a question, the script answers it calmly.
- Never promise a result ("Sembuh dalam 1 minggu").

## Script skeleton (60–90 s)
| Time | Beat | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook** | One line from above. On-screen text = hook. |
| 3–10 s | **Stakes** | Why this matters to the viewer / family. |
| 10–50 s | **The 80/20** | 2–3 points max. One plain-language explanation per term. |
| 50–70 s | **Red flags / action** | When to see a doctor; what to ask. |
| 70–90 s | **Close** | One-line takeaway + soft CTA ("Simpan video ni", "Follow untuk bahagian 2", or booking only if requested). |

## Pacing
- BM ≈ 2.0 words/s, EN ≈ 2.3 words/s → 60 s ≈ 120 BM / 140 EN words; 90 s ≈ 180 / 200.
- One idea per sentence. Cut every 3–5 s (B-roll, zoom, text change).
- On-screen text ≤ 6 words per card.
