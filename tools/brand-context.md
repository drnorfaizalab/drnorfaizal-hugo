# DR NOR FAIZAL: BRAND CONTEXT

MBBS (Malaya) · MS (Universiti Malaya) · DPhil (Oxon). Oxford-trained. He works in batik scrubs, not a white coat.

Write the hospital as a place he works, never as the speaker. Never write "KPJ Tawakkal".

## 2. Pillars
- Brain: glioma, glioblastoma, meningioma, pituitary tumours, brain metastases, paediatric brain tumours, spinal tumours; awake craniotomy.
- Head: migraine, cluster headache, trigeminal neuralgia, occipital neuralgia, facial pain, cervicogenic headache; microvascular decompression, nerve blocks.
- Pain: slip disc, back pain radiating to the leg, neck pain, chronic pain; radiofrequency ablation, spinal cord stimulation, intrathecal drug delivery, spinal injections.
Content mix: Brain 40%, Pain and Head as the diagnosis gateway 25%, Philosophy and story 15%, Patient Q&A 12%, Thought leadership 8%.

## 3. Voice
- Human first, credentials second. Warm authority, never clinical distance, never corporate. Always the person, never the hospital.
- Specific: name the procedure, the nerve, the moment.
- Story earns trust before any ask. 80/20: keep only what drives understanding and the next action.
- Short paragraphs, active verbs. No bullet dumps in long-form.
- Hooks describe the patient's problem, 12 words or fewer, no greeting before the hook.
- Close calmly: red flags and when to see a specialist.

## 4. Bahasa and English
- Bahasa Malaysia: reach and patient connection (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, website default). Educated Malaysian friend: conversational but dignified, saya/anda. Not formal government BM, not Indonesian spelling. BM term first, English in brackets where needed: ketumbuhan otak (brain tumour).
- English: authority (LinkedIn, GPs, website /en/). Understated Oxford precision. British spelling: tumour, haemorrhage, paediatric.
- Adapt, don't translate.

## 6. Guardrails
- Never write: world-class, state-of-the-art, cutting-edge, holistic solutions, your health is our priority, best neurosurgeon, No.1, guaranteed, 100% safe, miracle, painless (as a promise), terbaik, dijamin, tanpa risiko.
- MMC and Medicine Advertisements Board: no superlatives, no comparisons, no fee discounts, no outcome guarantees, no before-and-after claims. Testimonials describe an experience, never a promised outcome.
- Public education only, never diagnosis of an unexamined case.
- No identifiable patient details without documented consent. Scans anonymised.
- MMC and NSR numbers appear on formal documents only.
