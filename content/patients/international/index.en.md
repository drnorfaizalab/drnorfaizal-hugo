# International Patients

I see patients who travel to Kuala Lumpur from Singapore, Indonesia, Australia, Canada and beyond. The usual reasons are a second opinion on a brain or spine tumour, headache or facial pain that has not settled with treatment at home, and chronic pain that may suit a minimally invasive procedure.

Consultations are in English or Bahasa.

---

## What I treat

- **Brain and spine tumours:** glioma, glioblastoma, meningioma, paediatric brain tumours
- **Headache and facial pain:** chronic migraine, cluster headache, trigeminal neuralgia
- **Chronic pain:** back, neck and nerve pain, treated with minimally invasive interventional procedures

See the full list on the [Expertise]({{< relref "/expertise" >}}) page.

## How to start, before you book flights

1. Write a short summary: your diagnosis (if known), when symptoms began, treatments so far, and current medication.
2. Attach your scans and reports (list below).
3. Send it to the clinic on WhatsApp or by email. The clinic team will guide you on the next step.

## What to send

- MRI or CT **images** (the DICOM disc or download link), not only the report
- Radiology reports
- Biopsy and pathology reports, including molecular results if done
- Operation notes and discharge summaries
- Radiotherapy and chemotherapy summaries
- Current medications and allergies

---

## Planning your visit

- **Where:** Tawakkal Specialist Hospital, Jalan Pahang, Kuala Lumpur. Tuesday and Thursday afternoons are at Ampang Puteri Specialist Hospital. See [clinic hours and directions]({{< relref "/contact" >}}).
- **Dates:** further imaging or tests may be needed after the first consultation. Keep your travel dates flexible where you can.
- **Who comes with you:** bring the original images, and a family member who can help you weigh decisions.
- **Fees and insurance:** ask the clinic team when you make contact, so you know what to expect before you travel.

## This page is not for emergencies

Sudden severe headache, a seizure, sudden weakness, loss of vision or loss of consciousness need urgent care. Call your local emergency number (999 in Malaysia) or go to the nearest emergency department now.

This page gives general information. It does not replace a consultation.
